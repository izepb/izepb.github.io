I am a Senior Scientist in the Multimodal Discovery Lab at [Exscientia/Recursion](https://www.exscientia.com/), based in Vienna, Austria. I specialize in computational biology and network medicine, with a focus on developing innovative approaches to understand complex diseases and discover novel therapeutic targets.

In my current role, I am building a patient-first framework leveraging Exscientia's primary multimodal data with goals ranging from understanding cancer biology to discovering next-generation targets. Working closely with bench scientists, we are optimizing efficient learning loops in sample-constrained settings.

I hold a PhD in Medical Informatics & Complex Systems from Medizinische Universität Wien, where I developed graph-based tools to maximize context relevance for multiple data resources before integration. My research focused on improving rare disease gene prioritization strategies and understanding underlying disease mechanisms through gene relationships from genotype to phenotype.

My research interests include network medicine, multimodal data integration, computational biology approaches to rare diseases, and systems biology. I have been recognized with several awards including winning the CytoData2019 hackathon and receiving the Outstanding Poster Presentation Award at the EMBO conference on network biology.
